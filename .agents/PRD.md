# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Project: Khalil & Sons - Luxury Jewellery Business Website

## Objective
Deliver a digital experience for Khalil & Sons luxury jewellery house, providing both public atelier exploration and comprehensive administration capabilities.

## Core Modules & Functionality
1. Public User Experience:
   - Hero Showcase, Heritage Story, Historic Atelier, and Featured Masterpieces.
   - Masterpiece Gallery with category navigation, pagination, and detail inspection.
   - Live Gold Rates display, calculator, and historical records.
   - Client Reviews and testimonial submissions.
   - Atelier Inquiries, Vault/Cart drawer, and bespoke appointment bookings.
   - Brand narrative (About Us) and interactive contact suite.
2. Back-Office Administration:
   - Secured Admin Dashboard with key business metrics.
   - Inventory & Gallery Management (CRUD).
   - Rate broadcast and history update management.
   - Reviews moderation and user role access governance.
   - Customer inquiry correspondence and follow-ups.
3. Reliability & Standards:
   - Zero placeholder/dummy data in production workflows.
   - Custom styled dialogs for confirmations and alerts.
